    Add Robinhood Chain

    Open MetaMask, Robinhood Wallet, or any EVM wallet. Quantum Tiger lives on Robinhood Chain, so the wallet has to be on that network before the swap.

    Get Robinhood ETH

    $QT is bought with ETH that already sits on Robinhood Chain. Bridge ETH over and keep a little extra for gas. The same Robinhood ETH pays for the swap and the network fee.

    The Arbitrum portal can send ETH to Robinhood Chain when you pick that network.

    Swap for $QT

    Press Buy Now. Uniswap opens on Robinhood with $QT set as the token you receive. Connect the wallet, enter how much Robinhood ETH to spend, and read the quote before you sign.
